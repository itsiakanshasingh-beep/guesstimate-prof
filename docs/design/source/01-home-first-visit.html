guesstimate professor

Guesstimate Professor trains you to estimate any unknown number with a clear structure, the way interviewers judge it: method first, number second.

How it works

  1. 1Pick a difficulty. You get one question.
  2. 2Work it out in your own words, typed or spoken. Ask for a nudge if you're stuck.
  3. 3Tick off the model structure, then see if your number is in the right ballpark.
